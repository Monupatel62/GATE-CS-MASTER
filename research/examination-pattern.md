# GATE Examination Pattern Research

## Official 2026 baseline
- Duration: 3 hours.
- Total marks: 100.
- General Aptitude: 15 marks.
- Subject component: generally 85 marks, with paper-specific compulsory and optional sections.
- Question types: MCQ, MSQ and NAT.
- Questions carry 1 or 2 marks.
- Incorrect MCQ answers attract negative marking: one-third of the mark for a 1-mark MCQ and two-thirds for a 2-mark MCQ.
- No negative marking for MSQ or NAT.
- No partial marking in MSQ.
- Some papers have optional or candidate-selected sections. Store these at section level.

## Paper-specific structures
The official 2026 pattern page identifies special structures for AR, GE, GG, XE, XH and XL. Record section identifier/title, compulsory or optional status, selection rules, marks and year-specific wording.

## Historical work required
Do not apply 2026 rules retrospectively. Build a year-wise timeline from official brochures and original papers covering duration, total marks, GA, question types, answer entry, negative marking, optional sections, sessions and sectional instructions.
