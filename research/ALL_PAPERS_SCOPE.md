# All-Papers Research Scope

## Scope
The master database covers every GATE paper code that can be evidenced from 1983 through 2026. CS/IT is the first pilot; after its extraction and verification workflow is validated, the same pipeline expands to every other paper code.

GATE 2027 information is a forward-looking reference only. A paper code newly listed for 2027 is not treated as a 1983–2026 historical paper.

## Code-list baseline
The official GATE 2026 site lists 30 test papers. The registry lists those codes and adds RA as a 2027-only code because it appears on the official GATE 2027 site. This is not a claim that every code existed in every year.

## Per-code research requirements
- First year offered, supported by official or institutional evidence.
- Last year offered, where applicable.
- Exact annual paper/session/set/section variants.
- Official question paper and answer-key URLs.
- Original PDF inspection, page count and file hash.
- Question count, marks and question-type structure.
- Syllabus version and changes.
- Missing/inaccessible years.
- Renaming, retirement, merger or replacement evidence.

## Historical caution
Do not assume current paper codes existed in 1983. GATE's first examination year does not prove that every present-day paper existed then. Blank introduction/retirement years mean research is pending.

## CS/IT pilot rules
- Preserve historical CS and IT labels exactly as printed.
- Do not merge IT-labelled papers into CS without evidence.
- Process one year/session paper at a time.
- Check visible paper code, session, PDF pages, question numbering and key version.
- Verify extracted text against the original PDF page.
- Keep official key answers separate from independent solutions.
