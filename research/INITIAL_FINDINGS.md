# Initial Research Findings

Research snapshot date: 2026-11-14

This document records findings from source pages inspected during the initial research pass. It is a leads-and-evidence report, not a claim that the question corpus is complete.

## Official archive findings

### GATE 2026 — IIT Guwahati
- Official master question paper and answer-key page: https://gate2026.iitg.ac.in/QPs-answer-keys.html
- The page lists separate Computer Science & Information Technology papers: CS-1 (Forenoon) and CS-2 (Afternoon).
- The page explains that the answer key order corresponds to the master question paper and warns that candidate-console question/option order may differ.
- It describes MCQ, MSQ, NAT marking rules and the MTA (Marks To All) notation.
- Official downloads page: https://gate2026.iitg.ac.in/download.html
- It advertises a bulk download of papers from 2007 to 2025 and year-wise papers from 2021 to 2025.
- These are confirmed archive coverage claims. Individual CS files, page counts, exact sessions and key files still need enumeration and verification.

### GATE 2025 — IIT Roorkee
- Official master question paper and answer-key page: https://gate2025.iitr.ac.in/question-papers.html
- The official page explains the master paper/key order relationship and the MCQ/MSQ/NAT answer rules.
- Individual CS-1 and CS-2 PDF/key links must be extracted and checked into the source registry.

### GATE 2024 — IISc Bengaluru
- Official site: https://gate2024.iisc.ac.in/
- Its downloads/archive pages expose master question papers and keys.
- The 2026 IIT Guwahati official archive page claims bulk papers from 2007–2025, and IISc's site should be used as an independent official archive reference.

### GATE 2023 — IIT Kanpur
- Official master papers and answer keys: https://gate.iitk.ac.in/GATE2023/papers_keys.html
- The page lists Computer Science and Information Technology (CS).
- Exact PDF and answer-key links must be captured and verified.

## Historical discovery leads

### PreviousYearPapers.in
- Index: https://www.previousyearpapers.in/GATE/
- Lists CS PDFs from 1991 through 2015, plus 2016 session files labelled S5 and S6.
- This is a third-party index. It establishes that files are listed there, not by itself that every listed file is complete, authentic, official, or all sessions.

### GATE Overflow
- Previous years: https://gateoverflow.in/previous-years
- Its index lists CS material from 1987 onward and labels additional older IT papers separately for some years (e.g. 2004–2008).
- It is a community resource useful for discovery and solution discussion, not an official answer-key authority.

### NPTEL
- Question paper map: https://gate.nptel.ac.in/gatequestionpaper.html
- Institutional discovery source. It should be checked for individual CS year/session coverage.

## Important modelling consequences
1. A year may contain multiple CS sessions/papers. The key must identify paper/session/set, not only year and question number.
2. Older archives may use labels such as CS and IT separately. Do not silently merge historical IT-labelled papers into the CS paper series.
3. Keep master question paper numbering and candidate response ordering distinct where source documents indicate differences.
4. Answer keys can include special dispositions such as MTA; the schema must preserve them.
5. For older papers without a located official key, use a pending or independently-solved answer status, never a fabricated official answer.
6. Archive range is not the same as question-level verified coverage.

## What has not yet been established
- The earliest year in which a GATE CS paper was actually conducted.
- Whether the 1987–1990 GATE Overflow entries are complete original papers and whether those years represent CS versus an earlier naming convention.
- Whether every year from 1991 to 2006 has exactly one paper/session.
- The complete count of papers, sessions, sets, questions, and official final keys.
- All historical syllabus versions and the effective years for each version.
