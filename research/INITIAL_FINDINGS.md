## Historical discovery leads (not primary proof)

### PreviousYearPapers.in
- Index: https://www.previousyearpapers.in/GATE/
- Lists CS PDFs from 1991–2015 and two 2016 files labelled S5 and S6.
- This proves only that the index lists those files; authenticity and completeness require PDF inspection.

### GATE Overflow
- Index: https://gateoverflow.in/previous-years
- Lists CSE items from 1987 onward and separately labels some historical IT papers.
- Community material is useful for discovery and solution discussion, not as the official answer-key authority.

### NPTEL
- Map: https://gate.nptel.ac.in/gatequestionpaper.html
- Institutional discovery source; inspect each linked document.

## Modelling consequences
1. Key question identity on year + paper/session/set + question number, not year + question number alone.
2. Preserve legacy labels such as CS and IT separately until their historical relationship is evidenced.
3. Preserve master paper ordering separately from candidate response ordering.
4. Store MTA and other key dispositions explicitly.
5. Mark answers pending if no official key is located; never invent official answers.
6. An archive range is not equivalent to question-level verified coverage.

## Not yet established

- Whether 1991–2006 index entries represent every paper/session.
- Total historical paper/session/question counts.
- Complete historical syllabus versions and effective years.
