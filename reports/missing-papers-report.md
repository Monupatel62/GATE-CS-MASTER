# Missing and Inaccessible Papers

**Status:** Historical inventory is incomplete.

For every missing item record:
- Examination year.
- Paper code and historical printed label.
- Expected session/set (if evidenced).
- Last known official source URL.
- Third-party discovery leads.
- Whether the PDF is missing, inaccessible, corrupt, incomplete or unverified.
- Search attempts and date.
- Next recovery action.

Do not label a paper as not conducted merely because no PDF was found.
